# Pilot operations (proposed)

Use this only as a preparation checklist; the software is not yet approved for real clinic operations. Identify a clinical reviewer, operational contact, manager and client-service representative. Verify the profile, hours, species, service capabilities, source labels and correction process with the clinic. Confirm staff accounts and complete an observed workflow before marking the pilot active.

The partnership workflow uses a proposed 90-day free pilot beginning on activation and a day-60 review. The example $249 monthly location price is a configurable hypothesis, not a validated price or charge. No billing is automated. Administrators can record an extension and a conversion agreement; these records do not collect payment. The workflow still requires broader operational verification.

For evaluation, record baseline and pilot periods, covered shifts, administrative call categories and measured handling time, clinical calls, confirmed arrivals, and data completeness. Distinguish attributed visits from clinic-estimated incremental visits. Never claim staff payroll savings or clinical outcomes from unvalidated assumptions.
